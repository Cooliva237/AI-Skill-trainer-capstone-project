Work is pulled from `docs/source/Project Backlog.pdf`. Epics that span more than one sprint stay open across those sprints. Item statuses are the statuses in the backlog, not a judgment of the code.

- Backlog: E0 (ongoing through Sprint 4), T0.1, T0.2

- Backlog: E0 (ongoing), T0.3
